Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Voice Khata

Voice Khata is built so your shop's financial records stay private and under your control on your own device.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Your customer list, khata entries, sales, expenses, and stock counts are stored locally inside your browser's IndexedDB vault encrypted with AES-GCM 256-bit encryption. The encryption key is generated on your device and marked non-extractable.

2. Voice Audio & Microphone Access

We only activate your microphone when you press or hold a microphone button. Audio stays strictly in memory while being transcribed and is immediately discarded after the response. Voice Khata never stores audio recordings or raw sign-in transcripts.

3. Passwords & Security Controls

Voice entry is never available on password, confirm-password, login code, or PIN fields. Those fields are typed only. API keys used for voice transcription or synthesis remain exclusively on the server proxy and are never sent to your browser.

4. Reminders & Sharing

Payment reminder opt-in toggles are disabled by default. When you tap Remind, Voice Khata opens your device's share sheet with a pre-filled polite message that you review and choose whether to send—nothing is ever sent automatically.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete all local ledger data and destroy your device encryption key at any time using the One-Tap Data Delete option inside Privacy & Vault in the app.

6. Contact

If you have questions about privacy, please reach out via our Contact page.
