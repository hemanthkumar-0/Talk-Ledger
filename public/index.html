Security & Privacy

Your books belong strictly to you.

Designed from the ground up to protect shop finances and customer privacy.

Encrypted Ledger (AES-GCM)

Entries and customer balances are encrypted at rest inside IndexedDB with a 256-bit non-extractable device key.

API Keys Never Exposed

Speech requests route through our server-side proxy so private keys and service credentials never touch your browser.

Data Stays on Your Device

Your ledger works directly from local storage without forcing cloud uploads. Voice audio is never saved.

Delete Anytime

One tap in Privacy & Vault permanently removes every customer, entry, and encryption key from your device.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my shop data safe and private?
Yes. Your customer accounts and ledger entries are encrypted on your own device using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption. Voice recordings are processed only while you speak and are never stored.
Does Voice Khata work offline?
Yes. Once installed or loaded, the app shell, local ledger, reports, and typing entry continue working offline. When internet connectivity is available, live cloud speech processing resumes automatically.
Which languages are supported?
The interface supports English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, and Urdu (with full right-to-left layout), and the voice ledger parser understands natural English and Hinglish phrases including everyday Indian number shorthand.
Is Voice Khata free?
Yes. You can open Voice Khata and manage your daily credit book, dues, profit and loss summaries, and stock counts completely free.
Do I need to sign up to start using it?
No signup is required. Click Get Started to open your blank khata immediately on your device. Optional account sign-in with a typed 4-digit shop PIN is available inside the app whenever you want it.
What if I speak the wrong amount by mistake?
Just say “No, 650” or “Make it 650” right after. Voice Khata updates the amount in place without creating a duplicate row and confirms the corrected figure aloud.
Can I install Voice Khata on my phone?
Yes. Voice Khata is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android Chrome tap “Add to Home screen”, or on iPhone Safari tap Share and select “Add to Home Screen”.
