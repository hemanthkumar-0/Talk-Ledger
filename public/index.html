Features

Everything your daily khata needs, hands-free.

Precision-built for busy counters where typing every rupee slows you down.

Tap & Push-to-Talk Voice

Tap once to speak or hold the dedicated gold voice medallion. Multi-alternative speech capture keeps entries sharp at noisy counters.

Automatic Ledger Parsing

Deterministically extracts amounts, credit sales, payments, relative dates like yesterday, and in-place corrections with zero duplicates.

Clear Spoken Replies

Reads back every saved amount and customer balance in one crisp sentence so you verify totals without squinting at the screen.

Multi-Language Support

Use the interface, voice recognition, and INR formatting in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, and Urdu with complete RTL layout support.

Encrypted Local Storage

Every balance is locked inside IndexedDB using a 256-bit AES-GCM non-extractable device key, with optional Supabase sync when signed in.

One-Tap Data Delete

Clear your entire local ledger and destroy the encryption key permanently in one tap whenever you want a clean slate.

Installable Offline PWA

Add Voice Khata straight to your home screen on Android or iPhone. Lightweight architecture stays smooth even on entry-level phones.

Dues, Profit & Stock Reports

Track overdue balances, opening and closing statements, daily profit and loss charts, and low-stock alerts in one crisp dashboard.

Favorite Customers & Filters

Star regular buyers to pin them at the top and filter your credit book or customer balances by Favorites, Owes, Settled, or Advance.

Security & Privacy

Your books belong strictly to you.

Engineered from first principles to safeguard shop finances and customer privacy.

AES-GCM 256-bit Vault Zero-trust device key · Row-Level Security

  • Encrypted Ledger (AES-GCM 256-bit)

    Entries and customer balances are encrypted at rest inside IndexedDB with a non-extractable device key.

  • API Keys Never Exposed

    Speech requests route through our server-side proxy so private credentials never reach your browser.

  • Data Stays on Your Device

    Works directly from encrypted local storage without forcing uploads. Spoken audio is never recorded or retained.

  • Delete Anytime in One Tap

    Permanently erase every customer, entry, report, and encryption key from Privacy & Vault whenever you choose.
